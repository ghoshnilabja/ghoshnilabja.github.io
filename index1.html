01 / About me

A little about me.

Hi this is Nilabja, a Kolkata-based data scientist who enjoys taking complex, ambiguous problems and finding a useful way forward.

I have done my Bachelor of Science in Physics at St. Xavier's College, Kolkata , followed by a master's in Big Data Analytics at RKMVERI. Along the way, I developed an interest in natural language processing and research on how AI understands language and bias.

I went on to work in quantitative research, exploring options volatility, systematic trading ideas and financial sentiment. Now-a-days, at EY, I develop machine-learning solutions for demand forecasting and business planning.

What connects these different chapters is curiosity: asking the right questions, experimenting with ideas, and building things that have a practical purpose.
